## 🛑 The Corporate Challenge: Why Enterprises Need Charlie's CRM

Modern enterprises and manufacturing/distribution companies face 4 major bottlenecks:

1. **Tool Fragmentation & Data Silos**: Sales teams use one CRM, service centers use another ticketing tool, and warehouse teams use an isolated ERP, leading to lost data and slow resolutions.
2. **Warranty Fraud & Counterfeit Leakage**: Without real-time serial authentication, companies lose 8–15% of annual revenue to fraudulent warranty claims and unauthorized channel sales.
3. **Unmonitored Field Operations**: Field sales and service agents lack real-time visit tracking, location check-ins, and automated order booking.
4. **Rigid SaaS Pricing & Lack of White-Labeling**: Legacy CRMs charge exorbitant per-user pricing with zero tenant-level customization or white-labeling capabilities.

## 🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security & Governance

Charlie's CRM is built on an authoritative **3-Layer Authorization & Data Isolation Model**:

```text
SUPER ADMIN (Platform Owner)
   │  Controls WHAT MODULES a tenant owns
   ▼
COMPANY ADMIN (Enterprise Customer)
   │  Controls WHO inside the enterprise accesses features
   ▼
ROLE & DATA SCOPE (Manager, Executive, Dealer)
   │  Controls WHICH RECORDS the user can view (Region, Self, etc.)
   ▼
BUSINESS ACTION (View, Edit, Approve, Export)
```

- **5-Strike Intelligent Account Lockout**: Blocks brute-force threats after 5 failed attempts (`HTTP 423 ACCOUNT_LOCKED`) with self-serve unlock controls for admins.
- **Granular Data Scoping**: Restrict sales managers to `REGION: West`, executives to `SELF`, and dealer managers to their assigned dealer networks.
- **Comprehensive Audit Trail**: Every privilege change, export, and status update is immutably logged with timestamp, user ID, IP address, and tenant context.
- **Soft Deactivation by Default**: Employee deactivations preserve all historical sales records, customer interactions, and audit data.
